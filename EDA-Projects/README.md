# Student Productivity and Digital Distraction Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing student productivity and digital distraction patterns using Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).

The main objective is to understand the relationship between study habits, sleep patterns, phone usage, social media usage, stress levels, and students' focus scores.

## 📊 Dataset Information

- **Dataset Name:** Student Productivity and Digital Distraction
- **Total Records:** 20,000
- **Total Columns:** 18
- **Dataset Type:** Student Productivity and Digital Distraction

## 🔍 Key Features

- Study Hours
- Sleep Hours
- Phone Usage
- Social Media Usage
- Stress Level
- Focus Score

## 📈 Key Findings

The correlation analysis revealed the following relationships with student productivity:

| Feature | Correlation |
|---|---:|
| Study Hours | +0.733 |
| Focus Score | +0.411 |
| Sleep Hours | +0.341 |
| Phone Usage | -0.327 |
| Stress Level | -0.197 |

### Key Insights

- Study Hours showed a strong positive correlation with productivity (0.733).
- Focus Score showed a positive correlation (0.411).
- Sleep Hours showed a positive correlation (0.341).
- Phone Usage showed a negative correlation (-0.327).
- Stress Level showed a negative correlation (-0.197).

## 🎯 Project Objective

To explore the relationship between students' study habits and digital distractions and identify patterns that may be associated with student productivity and focus.

## 📝 Conclusion

The analysis indicates that study hours have the strongest positive correlation with student productivity among the examined features. Sleep hours and focus score also show positive relationships, while phone usage and stress level show negative relationships.

These findings help provide insights into student productivity patterns and the possible impact of digital distractions.
